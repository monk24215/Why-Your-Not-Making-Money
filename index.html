You're not missing a secret.
You're missing the audience.

Marketing isn't selling. It's finding people who already decided what they want, then giving it to them exactly the way they want it.

Every sale comes down to three questions

1

What do they want?

Not what you think they should want. What they are already looking for, in their own words.

2

How do they want it?

The format, the price, the speed, the level of detail. Whatever they already buy looks like this.

3

What does it look like?

Show them the exact thing they pictured. If they can see themselves using it, the sale is already made.

Find it in three steps

Simple on purpose. Nothing to buy.

1

Find a marketplace where people are already buying

Steady sales and lots of reviews. Reviews mean real buyers.

2

Study one seller who is already winning

What they sell, where buyers find them, and the words they use.

3

Read their reviews, especially the 1-star ones

They show exactly what buyers wanted and didn't get. Write down their phrases. That's what you give them.

Identify the audience. Know what they want. Give it to them.
