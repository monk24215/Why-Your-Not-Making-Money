Why You're Not Making Money Yet

Five usual reasons:

  1. Training resources sucked
  2. The system isn't good
  3. The guru lied or tricked you
  4. The product was bad
  5. The advertising was bad

Most of the time the real reason is none of these: the audience wasn't already looking for what you were selling.

Before anything else:

  1. Locate your audience.
  2. Identify exactly what they're trying to buy.
  3. Find someone already selling it successfully.
  4. Check that you can access what you need affordably.

Then give them what they expect, where and when they expect it.

Step 1 (Quick 1-2-3): Find a marketplace where people are already buying. Look for steady sales and lots of reviews.

Step 2: Find one seller already winning there. Note what they sell, where buyers find them, and the words they use.

Step 3: Read that seller's reviews, especially the 1-star ones. Those show what buyers wanted and didn't get. Write down their exact phrases.

Step 4: Test with the smallest version: one product, one listing, one link. Spend little or nothing. Your only goal is $1.

Step 5: Check the result. No sales and no clicks? Wrong place or wrong audience, so go back to Step 2. Clicks but no sales? Right audience, adjust the offer using their words.
