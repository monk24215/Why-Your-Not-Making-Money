If you've tried and it hasn't worked, you weren't failing.

You were pointed in the wrong direction.

The courses, systems, and gurus usually skip the one part that matters most: the people you're selling to. When you know who already wants something, and how they want it, the selling mostly takes care of itself.

The whole idea

Find people who already want something. Give it to them exactly the way they want it.

What you'll leave with

A one-page audience card, in your own words. Here's an example of a finished one:

Who they are: People who just adopted a rescue dog

What they wish they'd gotten: "Something that actually stops the pulling without yelling at him"

How they want it: Short videos, low price, this week

What it looks like: A 5-day leash guide, one 3-minute video a day, under $20

That's it. Small on purpose.

How this works

A few easy questions, one at a time. "Not sure yet" is always an okay answer. About ten minutes, no signup, and nothing to buy.
