The Quick 1-2-3

Find buyers who already decided.

1

Find a marketplace where people are already buying

Look for steady sales and lots of reviews. Reviews mean real buyers. Skip anything with no sales history.

2

Find one seller already winning there

Note what they sell, where buyers find them, and the words they use.

3

Read that seller's reviews, especially the 1-star ones

Those show what buyers wanted and didn't get. Write down their exact phrases. That's your copy.

Test before you build

Your only goal is $1.

Test with the smallest version

One product, one listing, one link. Put it where the winner's buyers already shop. Spend little or nothing.

Check the result

No sales and no clicks? Wrong place or wrong audience. Go back to step 2 and pick a different winner. Clicks but no sales? Right audience. Adjust the offer using their words.
